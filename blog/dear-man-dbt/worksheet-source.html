DEAR MAN worksheet

DBT interpersonal effectiveness
Plan one conversation per sheet

Use DEAR MAN when you need to ask for something or say no. Write your own words for each step, then practice saying them out loud.

What to say

D
DescribeJust the facts of the situation. No judgments, no "always" or "never".
E
ExpressHow I feel or what I think about it. "I feel…", "I think…".
A
AssertAsk for exactly what I want, or say no clearly. Don't hint.
R
ReinforceWhat's in it for them if they say yes. Say thank you in advance.

How to say it

M
(Stay) MindfulThe one line I'll keep coming back to if we get off track or I'm attacked.
A
Appear confidentHow I'll hold myself: voice, eye contact, posture. No apologizing for asking.
N
NegotiateWhat I can offer or accept instead. A question to ask them: "What would work for you?"
